Hynch's Dispel Highlight

Never miss a dispel again. Dispel Highlight is a minimalist addon for World of Warcraft: Forever that shows you at a glance who in your group or raid has a debuff you can dispel.

When a party or raid member is afflicted, their frame gets a colored border so you can easily spot and dispel the affected person. The color of the border matches the debuff's magical school: magic, curse, poison, disease.

Features
Only what you can dispel. Debuffs you can’t remove are ignored, so a highlight always means you can act on it.
Sound alerts. Get an audio cue the moment a dispellable debuff lands. It plays once per debuff, so it won’t spam you.
Per-school sounds: Give Magic, Curse, Poison and Disease their own sounds, so you know what’s coming before you look. Choose from built-in presets, a SoundKit ID, or your own sound file.
Options panel: Turn the addon on or off, adjust border thickness, toggle the colored tint, and set your sounds, all in one place.
Minimap button: Left-click opens the options and right-click toggles the addon. Don’t want it? Hide it from the options.
Addon compartment support: Find Dispel Highlight in the built-in addon compartment menu, with the same quick controls.
Lightweight: No libraries and no clutter. It was built specifically for the default party and raid frames.

Getting started
Download and unzip, then place the DispelHighlight folder in you World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\.
Log in. The addon detects your class’s dispels automatically.
Type /dh to open the options, or /dh test to preview the highlight and sound.

Slash commands
Command	What it does
/dh	Open the options panel
/dh toggle	Enable or disable the addon
/dh minimap	Show or hide the minimap button
/dh sound [school] <preset, ID or path>	Set the default sound, or one school’s sound
/dh test [school]	Preview the highlight and sound
